# Support

- **Bug** (something broken or wrong): [open an issue](https://github.com/OpenADMET/challenge-data-checker/issues/new/choose).
- **Question, usage help, "how do I…"**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/OpenADMET/challenge-data-checker/discussions), or the OpenADMET community — see [openadmet.org](https://openadmet.org).
- **Security vulnerability**: do not open a public issue — follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This tool is provided as-is and maintained on a best-effort basis.
